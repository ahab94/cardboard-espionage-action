### Fixes
- **"I can't play in my browser":** the browser version needs **WebGL 2**. If your browser doesn't have it, you now get a clear help screen instead of an error. It explains how to turn on graphics acceleration or update your browser, and links to the free desktop downloads.
- **Tall phones:** the game now fills the whole screen. No more black bars above and below.
- The web build is locked to Godot's most compatible graphics mode.
