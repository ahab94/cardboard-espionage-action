**Play on your phone:** https://ahab94.github.io/cardboard-espionage-action/

### What's new: touch controls 📱
- **Virtual joystick** (bottom-left) with analog control: push a little to creep, all the way to run.
- **HIDE button** (bottom-right): hold it to drop into the box. It works while you steer with the other thumb.
- **Pause button** (top-left) opens mission select. Tap a mission to play it, and tap to continue after a mission.
- **Hold your phone upright** for a portrait layout: the camera follows the box, and the controls get their own space below the map.
- The controls only appear on touch screens. Keyboard play is unchanged.

### Install
- macOS / Linux: `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/cardboard-espionage-action/main/install.sh | bash`
- Windows: `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/cardboard-espionage-action/main/install.ps1 | iex`
