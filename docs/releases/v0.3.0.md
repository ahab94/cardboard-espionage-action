### What's new: music and sound 🎵
Every sound is original, synthesized from scratch by a script: oscillators and noise, no samples.

- **Adaptive music:** a quiet stealth loop during missions, with a synced layer of drums and creeping strings that swells as the detection meter fills. The mission-select screen has a calm theme.
- **Sound effects:**
  - an alert sting and the "?" when something spots you
  - soft cardboard footsteps and a thump when you hide
  - laser zaps that get louder the closer you are
  - drone hum that rises as one passes
  - a keycard chime, the vault door hissing open, and a mission-complete jingle
- **M** turns the music on and off.

### Install
- macOS / Linux: `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/cardboard-espionage-action/main/install.sh | bash`
- Windows: `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/cardboard-espionage-action/main/install.ps1 | iex`
