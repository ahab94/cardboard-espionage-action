The first public build of **Cardboard Espionage Action**: you're a cardboard box sneaking through 10 VR missions.

### What's in it
- 10 missions, each introducing something new: sweeping cameras, box hiding, pillar shadows, laser tripwires, patrolling guards, drones, a keycard vault, a spinning turret, a labyrinth, and a final exam with everything at once.
- A detection meter: moving in view gets you spotted fast, while a still box buys you time.
- Mission select with saved best times and unlocks.
- An autopilot proves every mission can be beaten without an alert.
